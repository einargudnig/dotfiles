# What each area is for

The nine ranges are shared across the three knowledge bases, but the *content* of an area
differs by entity — Reykjavík's 40s are vans and thermoboxes, Maul ehf.'s 40s are the
software behind them. Read the range as a question, and the entity as the context.

For which categories currently exist, read the live index in Notion. This file is about
meaning, which changes far more slowly than contents.

---

## 10-19 — Culture / Stemming

*Who works here and how we treat each other.*

Employment terms, onboarding, time off, compensation, privacy policy, company history,
environment and sustainability, education and mentoring. Maul ehf. also keeps the `13
Roles` and `14 Responsibilities` databases here, which is why role definitions live in the
10s even though they describe operational work.

**Edge:** training *for a specific operational role* usually belongs with that operation
(driver induction is 41), while general employee development is 12. The test is whether
someone outside that role would ever read it.

**Edge — which entity owns people topics?** Employees are the one subject that genuinely
exists in both trees, and the KB question is easy to get stuck on. In practice: employment
terms, roles and responsibilities are anchored in **Maul ehf.** (the `13`/`14` databases
live there and cover all entities), while the day-to-day personnel practice of the
Reykjavík operation — sick leave, terminations, the company doctor — is in **Reykjavík**,
which is also the only tree with a `64 Starfsmannamál`. If a people process is
group-wide policy it belongs in Maul ehf.; if it's how Reykjavík actually runs it, it
belongs there. When it's genuinely both, ask rather than writing it twice.

## 20-29 — Suppliers / Birgjar

*People and companies we buy from.*

In Reykjavík this is the biggest area in the workspace: restaurants as suppliers,
drivers as contractors, packaging, premises, banks, accounting, marketing agencies. In
Maul ehf. it's software services, domains, delivery platforms.

**Edge:** the same restaurant appears in 20 and in 30. Supply relationship → 20.
Commercial relationship → 30. If you can't tell, ask whether the document is about what
they give Maul or about what Maul gives them.

**Edge:** a supplier of a *good or service used in operations* (thermoboxes, snow
clearing, cleaning) is still a supplier. The operational practice that uses it is 40s. Two
pages, two areas, linked — that's the intended shape, not a compromise. The test: if you
switched vendor tomorrow, which page would you rewrite? Content that survives the switch
isn't supplier content.

## 30-39 — Customers / Viðskiptavinir / Workplaces

*People and companies we sell to.*

Workplaces on contract, individual eaters (matgæðingar), restaurants as commercial
partners, customer surveys and reviews.

**Edge:** in Maul ehf. this area means the operating companies — Reykjavík and Tiffin are
the software's customers. Don't file an end-customer document there.

**Edge:** this is where the documents-not-records rule bites hardest. One page per
workplace doesn't scale; a database does.

## 40-49 — Operations / Rekstur / IT Operations

*How the work actually gets done, day to day.*

Reykjavík: service desk actions, routes and delivery, menus, orders, accounting
processes, quality processes, packaging, regulation, planning. Maul ehf.: the software
equivalents — service desk tooling, fleet software, menu systems, billing runs, test and
release practice.

This is the area people navigate most, and the one where a misfile does most damage,
because these are the pages someone reads while doing a task under time pressure.

**Edge:** 45 (process we chose) versus 47 (regulation we must follow) in Reykjavík.

**Edge:** anything that is a *decision about* the operation rather than the operation
itself is 60s.

## 50-59 — Creativity / Sköpun

*Things we make.*

Product design, brand and visual identity, graphic design, published material,
organisation design, and in Maul ehf. software development practice.

**Edge:** the artefact is 50s, the campaign that uses it is 70s. Logo files: 52. The
autumn campaign that put the logo on a bus: 74.

## 60-69 — Governance / Stjórnun

*How the company decides things.*

Board, reports, recruiting, planning cycles, personnel decisions like terminations.

**Edge:** recruiting sits here rather than in 10-19 because it's a management process
rather than an employment condition. Once someone is hired, their terms are 10s.

## 70-79 — Growth / Vöxtur

*Getting and keeping customers.*

Sales process, pricing, discounts, tenders, marketing, market analysis, competitors,
sales pitch, campaigns, PR.

**Edge:** pricing appears here (70.02, how we price) and in the customer area (what a
specific customer pays). The general policy is 70s.

## 80-89 — Financials / Fjármál

*Money in and out.*

Financial analysis, payroll, expenditures, forecasts, statements, grants, funding and
investors.

**Edge:** the *process* of approving an invoice is 44 (operations); the *state* of the
company's finances is 80s. Ask whether it's a procedure or a position.

## 90-99 — Legal / Lög og reglur

*Obligations, structure and the record.*

Company structure and articles, contracts, public authorities, asset registration, legal
cases.

**Edge:** a contract with a supplier is 91, not 20-29 — the supplier page describes the
relationship, the contract area holds the instrument. In practice Maul often keeps the
commercial detail on the supplier page and the signed document in Drive; that's fine, as
long as the supplier page points at it.

**Edge:** asset registration (93) is a register, not a document set. It behaves like a
database and is treated as one.

## Cross-entity divergences worth knowing

These are places where the same number means different things, and where a
straightforward-looking placement can land in the wrong tree:

- **30-39** is *Customers* in Maul ehf. and Reykjavík, *Workplaces* in Tiffin.
- **40-49** is physical operations in Reykjavík, software operations in Maul ehf.
- **47** exists only in Reykjavík and overlaps its own 45.
- **12** in Maul ehf. encodes a cross-reference in the second pair (`12.71 Marketing
  Learnings` points at area 70-79). Deliberate, and unique to that KB.
- **Tiffin** has categories numbered outside their area (`50 Daily Operations` inside
  40-49) and unnumbered ones (`Food safety / Egenkontrol`, `LEARNINGS🧠`). It's a
  historical tree — read placements there as artefacts rather than as precedent.

When you're about to place something by analogy with another KB, check that the analogy
holds. `41` meaning "delivery" in all three is real parallelism worth preserving; `40-49`
meaning the same kind of work is not.
